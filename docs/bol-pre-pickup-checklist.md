# US LTL Pre-Pickup BOL & Pallet Audit Checklist

Use this quick warehouse dock checklist before the LTL driver signs the Bill of Lading:

1. **Measure Outside-Most Dimensions (Include Pallet & Overhang)**: Capture L × W × H in inches including the 48"×40" wood skid (usually ~5–6") and any carton overhang.
2. **Verify Gross Weight on a Calibrated Floor Scale**: Include pallet tare weight (~35–50 lbs for standard GMA wood pallet) and shrink wrap/strapping.
3. **Check 2026 13-Tier PCF Density**: `PCF = Weight (lbs) / ((L × W × H) / 1728)`. Reference: [2026 Freight Class Chart](https://freightclasskit.com/freight-class-chart).
4. **Watch the 96" "Do Not Stack" Trap**: Adding a pyramid cone or "Do Not Stack" sticker can trigger 96" trailer-height dimensioning at the carrier terminal, cutting PCF in half (e.g., 48×40×42" @ 720 lbs jumps from Class 70 to Class 125). Test first: [96-Inch Rule Guide](https://freightclasskit.com/guides/do-not-stack-rule).
5. **Check Linear Feet on Multi-Pallet Orders**: Orders occupying ≥12 linear feet in a 53-ft trailer often trigger carrier Linear Foot Rules. Verify layout: [Linear Feet Calculator](https://freightclasskit.com/linear-feet-calculator).
6. **Print Clean Code 128 Straight or VICS BOL**: Generate print-ready 8.5×11" BOLs free at [FreightClassKit BOL Generator](https://freightclasskit.com/bol-generator).
7. **Snap 2 Dock Photos**: One with a tape measure showing height/width, one showing scale readout — essential if you later need to contest a carrier W&I reweigh invoice at [Reweigh Dispute Workbench](https://freightclasskit.com/reweigh-dispute).
